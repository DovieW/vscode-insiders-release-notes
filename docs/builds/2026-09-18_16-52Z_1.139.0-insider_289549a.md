## Summary

- Sessions: per-item Remove for recorded reference/artifact pills in the Agents window, so you can delete a single stale entry without breaking the underlying file/resource or other associations.
- Sessions onboarding: optional workspace selection plus a selectable workspace+model step in the default V2 flow (controlled by `onb.newSessionViewV2.variation`).
